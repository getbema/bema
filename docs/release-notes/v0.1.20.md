Bema 0.1.20 lets you show something else mid-talk and come back to your slides.

### New
- During a show, the new button next to **End show** (or **Slideshow → Show Something Else**, ⇧⌘S) takes the slides off the audience display, so a browser or demo you drag there is what your call sees. Share the whole display in Teams, Zoom or Meet for this.
- The timer keeps running and Bema remembers the slide. **Resume** (⌘↩) starts the slideshow again right where you left it.

### Updating
Bema offers this update automatically within a day, or choose **Bema → Check for Updates…**.
